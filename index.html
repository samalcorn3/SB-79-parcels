SB 79 Parcel Lookup

Transit-oriented development standards for every parcel in California’s urban transit counties, checked live against MPO, county, and HCD sources.

…or click any lot on the map. Zoom in to see every parcel colored by TOD zone.

How this works

Nothing is pre-downloaded. Each lookup queries, at that moment:

  • TOD stops and zones from each MPO’s SB 79 map (SCAG, MTC/ABAG, SANDAG, SACOG). Under Gov. Code § 65912.160(f) these maps carry a rebuttable presumption of validity.
  • Parcels from each county’s assessor or GIS parcel service.
  • City and county boundaries from the California Department of Technology.
  • Local exclusion layers that cities have published (e.g. San Francisco, Los Angeles).

Local ordinances, TOD Alternative Plans, and HCD’s review letters are tracked in a curated registry with a source for every entry. HCD publishes its letters only as PDFs on a Power BI dashboard, so the registry is maintained by hand and checked daily.

Informational only — not a zoning determination or legal advice. Confirm with the local planning department before relying on it.
